Resolution mode

Browsers cannot walk the delegation chain themselves. This asks NS for each zone from root down through your resolver, which gives real data, but the resolver does the recursion, not us.

Queries Google and Cloudflare in parallel and flags whether their answers match. The resolver picker is ignored in this mode.

DNSSEC

"DNSSEC OK" asks the resolver to include signature records and report validation. Without it the AD indicator can't be trusted.

"Checking Disabled" returns the answer even if DNSSEC validation would fail. Handy to tell a broken signature (SERVFAIL, then works with CD) from a dead server.
